1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & CORE OBJECTIVES
====================================================================================================
SENTINEL AI is an enterprise-grade CCTV video surveillance analysis, automated entity tracking,
and digital forensics platform. Modern CCTV environments suffer from camera overload, fragmented
footage, lack of real-time multi-camera tracking, and cumbersome manual review processes.

SENTINEL AI addresses these core operational challenges:

1. AUTOMATED INGESTION & OBJECT DETECTION:
   Processes raw CCTV video footage (MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV) across camera nodes, detecting humans,
   vehicles (cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks), backpacks, and faces with high accuracy.

2. MULTI-OBJECT TRACKING (MOT):
   Maintains persistent tracking identities for entities within a video stream across frames,
   eliminating redundant counts and rendering visual motion trails.

3. CROSS-CAMERA PERSON RE-IDENTIFICATION (Re-ID):
   Links sightings of the same person across disjoint, non-overlapping camera feeds using deep CNN
   appearance embeddings and spatial-color histograms.

4. SPATIAL TOPOLOGY & TRAJECTORY MAPPING:
   Maps camera positions onto building blueprints/floorplans. Calculates dwell times, transit
   velocities between zones, and reconstructs a subject's physical path through a facility.

5. LEGAL CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY EVIDENCE VAULT:
   Computes SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for all uploaded footage and video clips to ensure forensic
   integrity and detect tampering or unauthorized modifications.

6. CASE MANAGEMENT & EXECUTIVE REPORTING:
   Provides case dossiers, automated conversion of trajectory waypoints into structured investigation
   timelines, and one-click generation of printable executive forensic reports.

5. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE
====================================================================================================

5.1. OBJECT DETECTION ENGINE (Ultralytics YOLO)
- Architecture: YOLO (YOLOv8 / YOLO11 nano weights: `yolo11n.pt`)
- Execution: Frame sampling at configurable rates (default 5 FPS) to maximize processing throughput.
- Target Classes:
  * Person (Pedestrians, suspects, employees)
  * Vehicles: Car, Motorcycle, Bus, Truck
  * Accessories: Backpack, Handbag, Suitcase
- Output: Normalized bounding box coordinates [x, y, w, h], class labels, and confidence probabilities.

5.2. MULTI-OBJECT TRACKING (MOT)
- Core Algorithm: ByteTrack integrated with YOLO `model.track(persist=True, tracker="bytetrack.yaml")`.
- Resilient Fallback: `FallbackMOTTracker` implemented in pure NumPy/OpenCV:
  * Calculates Intersection-over-Union (IoU) between active tracks and new detections.
  * Tracks centroid motion history across up to 40 previous frames.
  * Handles temporary occlusions (up to `max_lost_frames` grace period).
- Visual Annotation:
  * Persistent ID badges: `[ID #12 | PERSON 88%]`.
  * Dynamic motion trail rendering: trailing bezier-style velocity lines fading behind moving targets.
  * Distinct color hashing: consistent colors assigned to each track ID across frames.
- Entity Thumbnail Cropping:
  * Automatically evaluates each detected entity per frame.
  * Saves the highest-resolution, highest-confidence crop as the master track thumbnail.

5.3. FACIAL DETECTION & HARVESTING
- Algorithm: OpenCV Haar Cascade Classifier (`haarcascade_frontalface_default.xml`).
- Optimization:
  * Throttled execution: activated exclusively on upper 45% of detected "person" crops.
  * Limits face harvesting to 2 high-quality face crops per unique track to prevent duplicate clutter.
  * Stores harvested faces in `storage/face_crops/` with frame and track references.

5.4. DEEP PERSON RE-IDENTIFICATION (Re-ID)
To link an individual appearing on Camera 01 (Entrance) with Camera 04 (Vault Exit), SENTINEL AI
extracts a rich 512-dimensional visual appearance signature:
1. Deep CNN Feature Pass (256 Dimensions):
   - Backbone: PyTorch MobileNetV3 Small (pretrained, classification head removed).
   - Input: Entity crop resized to standard Re-ID aspect ratio 224x112 (2:1 height-to-width).
   - Forward pass extracts high-level semantic features (body shape, clothing texture).
2. Multi-Part Spatial Color Descriptors (256 Dimensions):
   - Vertical partitioning:
     * Top Part (Head / Hat / Collar)
     * Middle Part (Torso / Shirt / Jacket)
     * Bottom Part (Trousers / Skirt / Shoes)
   - Feature Extraction: 16-bin Hue, 8-bin Saturation, 8-bin Value histograms computed for each part.
   - Converts to CIE Lab color space to ensure robustness against lighting and shadow variations.
3. Feature Fusion & Normalization:
   - Concatenates deep features (256-d) + spatial color histograms (256-d) = 512 dimensions.
   - Applies L2 vector normalization.
4. Metric Matching:
   - Evaluates similarity using Cosine Similarity:
     Similarity = (Vector_A . Vector_B) / (||Vector_A|| * ||Vector_B||)
   - Matches above threshold (e.g. >= 0.50 or >= 0.70) are flagged as identical subjects.

6. SPATIAL TOPOLOGY & CROSS-CAMERA TRAJECTORY RECONSTRUCTION
====================================================================================================

1. Coordinate Space:
   - Camera nodes are assigned normalized coordinates (X: 0–100%, Y: 0–100%) on building floorplans.
   - Direction angles (0–360 degrees) depict camera field-of-view cones.

2. Camera Network Topology (`camera_connections`):
   - Defines physical corridors, stairwells, and walkways connecting camera pairs.
   - Stores estimated transit times (e.g. 45 seconds between Gate South and Main Lobby).

3. Trajectory Assembly (`TrajectoryService`):
   - When a track or subject is queried, the system retrieves all cross-camera Re-ID matches.
   - Orders sightings chronologically by real-world timestamps or video offsets.
   - Filters out physically impossible leaps (e.g. traversing 500 meters in 2 seconds).
   - Computes:
     * Dwell Time: Duration the entity was visible under each camera's field of view.
     * Transit Time: Travel duration between consecutive camera zones.
     * Estimated Velocity: Physical movement speed between zones.
   - Generates an automated forensic narrative:
     "Subject entered via Main Entrance South (CAM 01) at 14:02:10. Dwelt 35s.
      Transited corridor in 42s to Lobby Central Corridor (CAM 02).
      Exited toward Vault Perimeter Exit (CAM 04) at 14:05:15."

4. Case Dossier Sync:
   - With one click (`POST /api/v1/trajectories/sync-case/{case_id}`), all trajectory waypoints
     are automatically converted into structured `InvestigationEvent` entries on an active case.

7. DIGITAL FORENSICS, EVIDENCE VAULT & SECURITY
====================================================================================================

1. Cryptographic Chain-of-Custody:
   - Upon upload, raw CCTV video files are digested using SHA-256 block-wise hashing:
     `hashlib.sha256().update(chunk)`
   - Hash is stored permanently in the `evidence` and `videos` database records.

2. Tamper Detection:
   - Investigators can trigger live verification (`POST /api/v1/evidence/{id}/verify`).
   - The system re-reads the physical video file from disk, recalculates the SHA-256 hash in real time,
     and compares it to the registered fingerprint.
   - If a single byte has been modified, corrupted, or replaced, the system marks `is_tampered = True`
     and sounds a high-priority security alarm in the UI.

3. Chain-of-Custody Audit Log:
   - Every verification, viewing, case linkage, or export event is timestamped and recorded
     in `chain_of_custody_log` JSON with the user ID, client IP, action, and resulting checksum.

4. Password Security & Authentication:
   - High-entropy admin credentials: `S3nt1n3l#Analytics2026!Key`.
   - Direct bcrypt password hashing with 12 salt rounds (`bcrypt.hashpw` / `bcrypt.checkpw`).
   - Stateless JWT tokens (HS256) with configurable expiration (1440 minutes / 24 hours).
   - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): ADMIN, INVESTIGATOR, VIEWER.

10. END-TO-END WORKFLOWS & INVESTIGATIVE SCENARIOS
====================================================================================================

SCENARIO 1: INGESTING NEW CCTV FOOTAGE
1. Navigate to CCTV Monitoring (`/cctv`).
2. Click "Upload Footage".
3. Select an MP4 video file and pick the target camera (e.g. `CAM 01 - Main Entrance South`).
4. Enter the recording date and time. Click "Upload & Process".
5. The backend calculates SHA-256 checksum, saves raw video into `storage/videos/`, samples frames
   at 5 FPS, runs YOLO detection + ByteTrack MOT, annotates bounding boxes and motion trails,
   crops entity thumbnails, harvests faces, and outputs an annotated video to `storage/processed/`.

SCENARIO 2: LOCATING A SUSPECT ACROSS MULTIPLE CAMERAS (Re-ID)

11. DEVELOPMENT HISTORY, BUG FIXES & COMPATIBILITY RESOLUTIONS
====================================================================================================

During development and environment testing, several critical engineering hurdles were solved:

1. PYTHON 3.14 & PASSLIB INCOMPATIBILITY:
   - Issue: The legacy `passlib` library crashed on modern Python releases during bcrypt initialization
     due to internal inspection of deprecated crypt symbols.
   - Resolution: Removed `passlib` dependency and implemented direct `bcrypt` hashing in
     `backend/app/core/security.py` using `bcrypt.hashpw(password.encode(), bcrypt.gensalt())`
     and `bcrypt.checkpw()`.

2. BREACHED CREDENTIAL ROTATION:
   - Issue: Static default credentials were identified as a security risk.
   - Resolution: Updated seed scripts and frontend login defaults to a high-entropy secret key:
     Username: `admin` | Password: `S3nt1n3l#Analytics2026!Key`.

3. OPENCV VERSION COMPATIBILITY & HAAR CASCADE PINNING:
   - Issue: Newer OpenCV releases (5.x preview) altered cascade classifier imports.
   - Resolution: Standardized on `opencv-python-headless >= 4.8.0` and implemented automated
     fallback downloading and local caching of `haarcascade_frontalface_default.xml`.

4. FRONTEND AXIOS INTERCEPTOR RELOAD LOOP:
   - Issue: Failed login attempts returned HTTP 401, causing the global response interceptor
     to trigger a full page reload loop on the `/login` route.
   - Resolution: Added route exception checking `!error.config?.url?.includes('/auth/login')`
     to permit graceful UI error toasts without page refreshes.

5. FAST VIDEO INGESTION & TRACKING PERFORMANCE:
   - Issue: High-resolution CCTV streams (1080p / 4K) at 30 FPS caused processing bottlenecks.
   - Resolution: Added intelligent frame sampling (default 5 FPS), PyTorch CPU thread optimization,
     and ByteTrack tracking persistence with `FallbackMOTTracker` to maintain real-time speed.
